# Slice 05 (ranges / levels / tests) — CATALOG rows

New rules, in `rules/CATALOG.md` table format.

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CloseRangePositionRun` | Entry | mirrored | `runBars - 1` | `runBars(3)`, `threshold(0.75)`, `epsilon(0.000001)` | EVERY one of the last `runBars` bars closed in the top `threshold` of its own high−low range (short: the bottom) — Grimes's buying- or selling-climax run of bars closing at their extreme (G03-06, cited twice). Written as `CountIF((Close − Low) > threshold × (High − Low + epsilon), runBars) >= runBars`: `CountIF` counts bars 0..N−1 INCLUDING the current bar and its condition is a true SERIES whose offsets shift per counted bar, so the unshifted condition counts this bar and the `runBars − 1` before it, and the count can never exceed `runBars` — the all-of-N test without a float equality (`ExitOnFirstOppositeBodyAfterRun`'s spelling, and EL does not short-circuit anyway). PRODUCT form, never `CloseRangePosition`'s quotient, so a zero-range bar cannot divide by zero; `epsilon` is that rule's book literal and in product form only makes a doji fail both sides. The SHORT is the exact mirror (`High − Close` against the same threshold), not `< 1 − threshold`, which differs from the mirror by the epsilon term. Not `CloseRangePosition`, its single-bar form (`ConsecutiveClosesBeyondEmaRun` beside `BarsBeyondEmaCount` is the precedent for keeping a run apart); not `StrongBodyBarCountInWindow`, which counts BODIES K-of-N |
| `SpringWithMinimumPenetration` | Entry | mirrored | `max(rangeLookback, atrLength + 1)` | `rangeLookback(20)`, `minPenetrationAtr(0.25)`, `atrLength(14)` | SPRING (long): the bar's low trades below `Lowest(Low[1], rangeLookback)` by at least `minPenetrationAtr` ATRs AND the bar closes back at or above that level; mirrored, the short is Grimes's upthrust at range resistance. Nine mined restatements of one same-bar test (G01-11, G02-03, G04-03, G05-08, G06-05, G06-46, G10-11, G16-04, G16-18). THE FLOOR IS THE POINT: `SmallBreakoutTrapReversal` CAPS the same penetration (Brooks's one-tick trap) where this FLOORS it — opposite bounds on one event, so neither is the other at any parameter, and at `minPenetrationAtr(0)` this is that rule with its cap disabled (merge decision 11). The level is the one-bar-shifted window extreme with an ATR-fraction margin, never ticks (merge decision 7), and beat G16-04/G16-18's swing pivot on mechanizability. The window offset is on the PRICE SERIES (`Lowest(Low[1], n)`, the `ReversalShortOfPriorExtreme` form) rather than `Lowest(Low, n)[1]`, which would read a function's own history. One `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange` call site, in the hook |
| `BodyExceedsAverageBarRange` | Entry | same | `lookback` | `lookback(20)`, `bodyMultiple(1.0)` | The bar's body `AbsValue(Close − Open)` exceeds `bodyMultiple` × the mean high−low RANGE of the `lookback` bars BEFORE it (G05-10) — Grimes's strong-momentum rejection bar, the bar that closes back across a broken level, and the momentum half of his failure test (the other half is a separate pane). Not `BodyAboveAverageMultiple`, which sizes the body against the mean BODY of its window and demands a with-trend colour: measuring a body against other bars' RANGES appears nowhere in the catalog and is a far harder test at the same multiple. Direction-agnostic, so both sides are identical text. The benchmark EXCLUDES bar 0 (`LargeOutsideBar` / `BodyAboveAverageMultiple` convention), and the mean is an explicit newest-first summation in both twins rather than `Average(High − Low, n)[1]`, because reading a function call's own history at an offset is unmeasured (`PullbackRangeBelowLegRange` is the precedent) |
| `NetMoveBelowAtrMultiple` | Entry | same | `max(lookback, atrLength + 1)` | `lookback(20)`, `netAtr(1.0)`, `atrLength(14)` | The ABSOLUTE net change in the close over the last `lookback` bars is below `netAtr` × ATR — no directional progress, the second half of Grimes's random-walk trading-range read (G04-10, merge decision 8). G04-10's containment half IS `RangeWidthBelowAtrMultiple` at its own defaults, so only the net-progress term is carried and the two AND in one pane to give the full test; the pair is not redundant, since a slow one-sided drift is TIGHT by the envelope rule and WIDE by this one. NOT `Momentum` negated: that rule is one-sided and mirrored, so its negation is still one bound on one side, while this is a two-sided band with IDENTICAL text on both sides. Decision 8 settles it as its own file rather than a second bound on `Momentum`, the band being direction-agnostic where `Momentum`'s bounds are not. One `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange` call site, in the hook |
| `LevelConfluenceAcrossWindows` | Entry | mirrored | `max(shortLookback + longLookback, atrLength + 1)` | `shortLookback(20)`, `longLookback(100)`, `tolAtr(0.5)`, `atrLength(14)` | The support level of a RECENT window sits within `tolAtr` ATRs of the support level of an OLDER, LONGER one (G16-20) — Grimes's confluence, levels from two scales lining up at one price; mirrored on the highs for resistance. Nothing in the catalog compares two LEVELS to each other — `NearAnyLevel` ORs the distance from PRICE to three magnets, a different question. The multi-timeframe read is two lookbacks on one series (merge decisions 4 and 15), 20 against 100 being the 5× multiplier. THE WINDOWS MUST NOT OVERLAP: a nested long window's low is at or below the short window's and the test would fire trivially, so the older window is offset past the recent one — bars 1..`shortLookback` against bars `shortLookback+1`..`shortLookback+longLookback`, the `ReversalShortOfPriorExtreme` construction, with the offset applied to the price series rather than to the result of `Lowest`. Both levels are one-bar-shifted window extremes and the tolerance is an ATR fraction, never ticks (merge decision 7); 0.5 ATR is that decision's magnet tolerance |
| `SessionOpenPositionInRange` | Entry | mirrored | `1` (plus the session state carried in locals) | `highFraction(0.8)` | The session's OPENING price sits in the top `highFraction` of the session's range SO FAR (long), the bottom fraction (short) — Grimes's O%Rng (G08-27, G16-13, two restatements of one glossary metric). The book's full-session range is knowable only in hindsight, so the range TO DATE is used, as G08-27 itself proposes; unlike `SessionOpenNearSessionExtreme`'s monotone read, a new session high or low moves both sides of this comparison. Not that rule: it bands the open against the running LOW in ATRs and reads open-near-low as STRENGTH, this places the open inside the session range as a FRACTION and reads the TOP as strength — a different tolerance basis AND the opposite polarity, which no negate or flip toggle reaches (merge decision 12). Session model per the standing decision (`date > date[1]`, running open/high/low in locals reset on the change); intraday bars only. Written MULTIPLIED, never as a ratio, so a session whose high equals its low fires both sides rather than dividing by zero; on the session's FIRST bar the running extremes are that bar's own, so the test is that bar's open position in its own range |
| `MomentumBeyondAtrMultiple` | Entry | mirrored | `max(lag + searchBars + lookback, atrLookback + 1)` | `lookback(3)`, `minMoveAtr(2)`, `atrLookback(14)`, `lag(0)`, `searchBars(0)` | The close `lag` bars ago is above the close `lookback` bars before THAT one by at least `minMoveAtr` ATRs (short: below it by as much), satisfied anywhere in the last `searchBars + 1` bars. Three Grimes readings in one rule (authored 2026-09-22 as inputs on `Momentum`, split out 2026-09-27 so `Momentum` stays the plain test): `minMoveAtr`/`atrLookback` is `MomentumBeyondAtrMultiple` (G02-06, "run up too far too fast", his 2 ATRs over 3 bars — the defaults); `lag` is `PriorLegDirection` (G02-01, the direction of the leg that ran INTO the window; his accumulation read is this rule NEGATED at `minMoveAtr 0` — the `LaggedHigherHigh` precedent); `searchBars` is `ImpulseLegNetMove` (G03-15, "trade only pullbacks preceded by significant momentum", about 15 bars — the impulse leg need not end on this bar). At `lag = searchBars = minMoveAtr = 0` it is `Momentum` term for term. ONE ATR read for the whole scan, at the current bar — MW's rolling `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange`, never a lagged read, which has no register row (`QuietBarCountInWindow`'s arrangement) — and read unconditionally so both twins advance the accumulator on the same bars. Max Bars Back must cover `atrLookback + 1` even when `lookback` is short |
| `TightRangeRunLength` | Entry | same | `max(rangeLookback + consecutiveBars - 2, atrLookback + 1)` | `rangeLookback(20)`, `rangeAtrMultiple(4)`, `atrLookback(14)`, `consecutiveBars(18)` | `RangeWidthBelowAtrMultiple` held for a run: the height of the last N bars (highest high − lowest low) is no more than K × the N-bar ATR on each of the last `consecutiveBars` bars — Grimes's consolidation that has lasted markedly longer than the trend's usual pauses (A-list key, G10-07, merge decision 10). Authored 2026-09-22 as a `consecutiveBars` input on `RangeWidthBelowAtrMultiple`, split out 2026-11-06 once templates placed that rule (the `MomentumBeyondAtrMultiple` precedent). `consecutiveBars` is the CONSOLIDATION LENGTH: Grimes wants a consolidation three or four times the trend's usual five-to-eight-bar pause, and since tracking prior consolidation lengths needs machinery no source describes, the ratio is baked into a fixed minimum of about 18 bars, the default. AT `consecutiveBars(1)` THE OUTER LOOP RUNS ONCE ON THE CURRENT BAR AND THIS IS EXACTLY `RangeWidthBelowAtrMultiple` AT `lag(0)` — the window is bars 0..`rangeLookback`−1 and `not (width > K × ATR)` is `width <= K × ATR` under the same tolerance (`MomentumConsecutiveBars` / `StrongTrendBarRun` precedent for a run count). ONE ATR value for the whole run, read at the current bar, MW's rolling `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange`, never a lagged read of it. The EL twin spells the window extremes as the same explicit nested loop the C++ uses, because the run's SHIFTED window has no registered function spelling — `Highest(...)[kk]` reads a function's own history at a loop-variable offset and `Highest(High[kk], ...)` calls a function inside a loop, which no rule in the corpus does; the loop reproduces `Extremes` exactly. Not `AdxBelowThreshold`: ADX can sit low inside a slow one-sided drift that this rule calls wide |

| `CloseNearSessionExtreme` | Entry | mirrored | 1 | `nearExtremeFraction(0.2)`, `strengthMode(0)` | At `strengthMode(0)`, the default and the original rule, the close sits in the bottom `nearExtremeFraction` of the day's range so far (long) / the top fraction (short) — the session-scoped range-position test as a FADE read, which is how Brooks states it for trading-range days.
