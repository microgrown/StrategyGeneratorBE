## Notes for the orchestrator

- **Input counts.** `BreakoutBaseEntryTemplate` and `Anti` carry **14 inputs each**, against a previous corpus maximum of 9 (`FinalFlagReversalBreakoutEntry`). Both were consolidated hard — one window input serving several parts, one `tolAtr`, one `atrLength`, one `lookback` — and 14 is what is left after that. **The design-grid pass must pin most of them**; the grid cannot afford to sweep these.
- **`FailureTest` not written** (see above). If Brian wants the named template on disk anyway, it is a two-part file with four inputs and no new machinery; say the word.
- **`BreakoutBaseEntryTemplate` uses `triggerMode`, not an OR, for the book's three entry triggers.** As bar tests they are NESTED (every spring bar also satisfies the base-floor touch) and G06-47 has no bar test at all, so the literal OR collapses to "true". They differ only in ORDER TYPE, which the engine does not model. Flag for Brian if a literal OR was wanted.
- **`FailedBreakoutTemplate` reads `Ticks[kk + jj]` at an offset**, which no existing rule in either corpus does — they all assign `vol = Ticks` at bar 0 and mirror the variable's history in a ring buffer. The `ticks` register row is VERIFIED and indexing a bar series word is ordinary EasyLanguage, but it is the one thing in this slice outside the corpus's established spelling, and it makes that rule's tick benchmark EXCLUDE the breakout bar where `BreakoutBarRangeAndVolumeExpansion`'s includes bar 0. Worth a look at the next MultiWalk comparison.
- **`Anti` leans on the `std::vector<double>` / `std::size_t` ring buffer**, so `validateRule` reports its standing "Unusual local type" warning — the same one `MomentumDivergenceAtSwingExtreme` and `BarRangeAboveStd` carry. No errors.
- **Grid floors from the `For` hazard.** Every scan loop in this slice is guarded in BOTH twins, but the grids must still start at: `NoSustainedPriorPenetration.preLookback` ≥ 1; `BreakoutBaseEntryTemplate.rangeBars`/`preLookback` ≥ 1; `FailedBreakoutTemplate.lookback`/`maxBarsSinceBreakout` ≥ 1 and `breakoutLookback` ≥ 2; `Pullback.pullbackBars`/`legBars`/`pullbackLookback` ≥ 1; `PullbackLowerTimeframeBreakout.pullbackBars`/`legBars` ≥ 1 and `equalBarsCount` ≥ 2; `Anti.lookback` ≥ 2, `failBars`/`maxWaitBars`/`spikeBars` ≥ 1.
- **`Anti.numLegs` is capped at 2** by the three-pivot scan (decision 14), so G06-34's three prior swings are out of reach; above the cap the rule simply never fires.
- **`Pullback`'s support level is the PULLBACK's own window extreme, not a swing pivot** — a stated departure from `LevelTouchCount`'s construction for the `PullbackSupportTestCount` part. The D mapping was made on the COUNTER, and G06-16 says "tests of support IN A PULLBACK"; decision 7 puts a confirmed pivot only where a level must persist from a break to a later retest, which this one need not.
- No rule in this slice was blocked on an unmeasured EasyLanguage word, and none needed an `ASSUMED` register row. `validateRule` and `lintElFeatures.py` are clean on all seven.
