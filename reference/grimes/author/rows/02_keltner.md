## Amended

| `EmaGapBar` | Entry | mirrored | `atrLookback + 1` | `emaLength(20)`, `marginAtr(0)`, `atrLookback(14)` | The bar does not touch the N-bar EMA: its low is above the average (long) / its high below it (short) — Brooks's "moving average gap bar", ten restatements of one single-bar test. Not `CloseAboveAverage`, which compares the CLOSE with its own SIMPLE average; this reads the bar's extreme against an EXPONENTIAL one, so it fires on different bars. `marginAtr`/`atrLookback` were added 2026-09-22 for Grimes's FREE BAR (G03-02, G07-06, G16-07; Grimes merge decision 1): they widen the average into a KELTNER BAND, so the whole bar must clear the EMA by `marginAtr` ATRs — the free bar is the gap bar measured against the band instead of against the line. At the default `marginAtr = 0` the margin is exactly 0.0, `emaVal + margin` is `emaVal` bit for bit, and every existing placement and template is unchanged; Grimes's own reading is `emaLength 20` / `marginAtr 2.25` / `atrLookback 20`, which is why the book's number is not the default. The pair is lifted character for character from `CloseBeyondEma`, the same band tested on the closing price, and the ATR is `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange`'s ROLLING accumulator with one call site in the hook — so the rule now reaches back `atrLookback + 1` even at the inert default. The EMA is `WFSafe_Xaverage`, MEASURED bit-identical to the plain `XAverage` built-in at a fixed length (`(wf − xa) · 1e12` exactly 0 on all 17,785 probed bars), seeded with the PRICE on the study's first calculated bar, recurrence `x + sf·(p − x)` as ONE statement with `sf = 2/(emaLength+1)`, and no warm-up hole. It never forgets its seed, so Max Bars Back and the chart start are part of the specification. `emaLength` may be optimized in grids (the wrapper recomputes SF on a length change, measured 2026-09-17) |
