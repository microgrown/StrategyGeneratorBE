# CATALOG rows — slice 10_exits (exits: targets, reward-to-risk, opposite-bar exits)

Twelve rules in the slice, twelve written, both twins each. Rows are in
`rules/CATALOG.md` table format, ready to append.

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MinRewardToRiskFilter` | Entry | same | 0 | `targetPoints(2)`, `rewardRiskMultiple(1)` | The trader's equation as a pre-trade gate: a fixed target clears K × the signal bar's own high−low range, which stands in for the planned risk because the stop sits just beyond the bar. Written as a product, not a ratio — EL raises a runtime divide-by-zero and a bar whose high equals its low is legal data. At a fixed `targetPoints` it degenerates to a maximum-signal-bar-range filter, which nothing else expresses: `BarRangeAboveStd` measures against a rolling mean and standard deviation, not an absolute distance. `targetPoints` is PRICE POINTS, not ticks (`MinMove`/`PriceScale` are UNKNOWN) |
| `RoomToRangeTargetFilter` | Entry | mirrored | `rangeBars` | `rangeBars(20)`, `minRoomPoints(2.5)` | There is at least X of room from the close up to `Highest(High[1], rangeBars)` — the far side of the block of N bars ENDING ONE BAR AGO — so the signal is not taken right under resistance; short is the mirror down to `Lowest(Low[1], rangeBars)`. The Extremes loop is seeded on the window's first bar with the tolerance-carrying strict comparison, as `StopBeyondRecentRangeExtreme` does. `minRoomPoints` is price points; the source's 10 ticks is carried as 2.5, ten Emini ticks |
| `BarHeightMeasuredMoveTarget` | Exit | mirrored | `refBar` | `refBar(1)`, `multiple(1)`, `tolerancePoints(0.25)` | Price reaches one bar's own high−low height projected beyond that bar's far extreme: long exits at `high ≥ refHigh + multiple × (refHigh − refLow) − tolerancePoints`, short mirrored on the low. The reference bar is read unconditionally (EL does not short-circuit, so Max Bars Back must cover `refBar` regardless) and the projection is FROZEN on the fill bar, where `BarsSinceEntry() = 0` makes `refBar = 1` the signal bar under the standing signal-bar-close convention — freezing is what holds the target still while the position is open. `PatternHeightMeasuredMoveTarget` with the window collapsed to one bar, kept separate because a one-bar height and a twenty-bar height are not the same bars |
| `EntryBarExtremeTarget` | Exit | mirrored | 1 | `tolerancePoints(0.25)` | Price reaches the far extreme of the bar the trade was entered from: the signal bar's high (long) or low (short), captured on the fill bar where `BarsSinceEntry() = 0` puts it at `[1]` (VERIFIED: 0 on the fill bar, 0 while flat, never a −1 sentinel). The prior bar is read every bar and only the freeze is conditional, so the deepest subscript stays 1 instead of the length of the longest trade. Brooks's first target after fading a countertrend bar; a fixed offset, not a pivot search, so deliberately not the same rule as a swing-extreme target |
| `ExitAtPriorRangeExtreme` | Exit | mirrored | `channelBars` | `channelBars(20)`, `tolerancePoints(0.5)` | Price reaches the far side of the channel — long exits when the high reaches `Highest(High[1], channelBars) − tolerancePoints`, short when the low reaches `Lowest(Low[1], channelBars) + tolerancePoints`, the window ENDING ONE BAR AGO. Same window arithmetic as `HighestHighBreakout` but in the Exit role and reaching the OPPOSITE extreme with a tolerance offset, so an Entry cannot stand in for it. `tolerancePoints` is price points; the source's 2 ticks is carried as 0.5 |
| `ExitOnFirstOppositeBodyAfterRun` | Exit | mirrored | `runBars` | `runBars(4)` | Every one of the `runBars` bars BEFORE this one closed above its own open and this bar closes below it — the first bear body after a bull run takes the long off; short is the mirror. `CountIF(Close[1] > Open[1], runBars)` counts bars 1..runBars, using the VERIFIED shifting-offset series semantics, and `≥ runBars` is the source's `= runBars` without a float equality since the count cannot exceed the window. Not `ConsecutiveCloseExit`, which fires on N consecutive rising CLOSES with no opposite-bar trigger; equals `ConsecutiveUpBars(runBars)` read at `[1]` plus a bear body at `[0]`, which an Entry rule cannot supply from the exit pane |
| `ExitOnOppositeStrongTrendBar` | Exit | mirrored | 0 | `bodyFraction(0.6)`, `tailFraction(0.2)` | One strong trend bar against the position closes it: long exits on a bear bar whose body is at least F of its own range with no more than T of that range left as a tail below the close; short is the mirror on a bull bar. The mechanizable half of Brooks's exit/reversal asymmetry — the always-in flip needs two such bars, the exit needs one. Body and tail as products, not quotients, so a zero-range bar cannot divide by zero |
| `ExitOnWeakFollowThrough` | Exit | mirrored | `rangeLookback` | `largeBodyFraction(1)`, `rangeLookback(20)`, `weakBodyFraction(0.3)`, `maxBarsAfterSpike(2)` | The position is at most `maxBarsAfterSpike` bars old, the PRIOR bar was a large trend bar in the trade's direction (body ≥ F × `Average(High[1] − Low[1], rangeLookback)`, the fresh plain `Average` over the window ending before it) and this bar's follow-through is weak — signed body under W of its own range, OR a close against the prior close. Not `WeakFollowThroughBarRequiresPullback`: that is an Entry, sizes "large" against the bar's OWN range, uses the absolute body and has no close-comparison or position-age clause. The signed body is deliberate — a bar closing against the trade is weak whatever its size |
| `FailedTargetReversalExit` | Exit | mirrored | 1 | `targetDollars(200)`, `toleranceDollars(12.5)` | Peak profit came within `toleranceDollars` of a fixed target and never filled, and the close is now beyond the prior bar's extreme (long: `close < low[1]`) — give up on the target and leave on the structure break. `MaxPositionProfit` is the INTRABAR peak of the favourable extreme (VERIFIED), which is precisely the "came within a tick of the target" reading since the limit would have filled on that extreme. Not `ProfitProtector`, which gives back a FRACTION of peak profit with no price-structure trigger. `toleranceDollars` defaults to one Emini tick |
| `GapMidpointMeasuredMoveTarget` | Exit | mirrored | `max(legLookback, 2)` | `legLookback(20)`, `useGap(0)`, `multiple(1)`, `tolerancePoints(0.25)` | Price reaches `mid + multiple × (mid − legStart)` — the measuring-gap projection, 2 × mid − legStart at multiple 1 — where `mid` is the breakout bar's own midpoint, or the true gap midpoint `(Low[1] + High[2]) / 2` when `useGap > 0`, and `legStart` is `Lowest(Low[1], legLookback)` (long) / `Highest(High[1], legLookback)` (short). The one formula serves both sides because `legStart` sits on the opposite side of `mid` for a short. Frozen at entry, unlike a target recomputed off a rolling midpoint every bar; the ingredients are read unconditionally so Max Bars Back covers them. `useGap` is a double toggle, as every input is a double |
| `PatternHeightMeasuredMoveTarget` | Exit | mirrored | `refBars` | `refBars(20)`, `multiple(1)`, `startFromOpen(0)`, `endAtClose(0)`, `tolerancePoints(0.25)` | Price travels a multiple of the entry-time N-bar high−low span beyond the broken extreme: capture `Highest(High[1], refBars)` and `Lowest(Low[1], refBars)` at entry, project `anchor + multiple × height`, exit when the high reaches it less the tolerance (short mirrored). Spike, channel, trading range, wedge, triangle, flag, double top/bottom and head-and-shoulders are all this arithmetic — the level broken IS the window's own extreme. Two double toggles give the book's alternate anchor pair: `startFromOpen` swaps the window's far side for `Open[refBars]`, `endAtClose` swaps the near side for `Close[1]` and anchors the projection there. Not `AtrProfitTarget`: the distance is the entry-time window range, frozen at entry, not the running ATR re-read every bar |
| `RewardRiskDeteriorationExit` | Exit | mirrored | 0 | `targetPoints(2)`, `stopPoints(2)`, `minRewardRiskMultiple(1)` | The trader's equation re-read every bar off the live close: remaining reward `EntryPrice + targetPoints − close` no longer covers K × remaining risk `close − (EntryPrice − stopPoints)`, with the risk still positive; short is the mirror. The exit-side counterpart of `MinRewardToRiskFilter` and the only continuous re-evaluation of the equation in the bucket — it fires mid-trade on a moving close, not once at the signal. The `MarketPosition` guard is load-bearing: `EntryPrice` reads 0 while flat and a back-adjusted series can carry negative prices. Distances are price points; the probability term of the book's equation is dropped, being uncomputable from bars |

## Notes for the orchestrator

- Every "ticks" quantity in the slice's pseudocode became a PRICE-POINT input,
  because `minmove` and `pricescale` are UNKNOWN in `rules/EL_FEATURES.md`:
  `toleranceTicks(1)` → `tolerancePoints(0.25)`, `toleranceTicks(2)` →
  `tolerancePoints(0.5)`, `minScalpTicks(10)` → `minRoomPoints(2.5)`. The
  conversions are at the Emini's 0.25 tick and are instrument-specific, the
  same convention slice 09 used for `PullbackNearMissesEntryPrice`.
- The four at-entry-capture rules (`BarHeightMeasuredMoveTarget`,
  `EntryBarExtremeTarget`, `GapMidpointMeasuredMoveTarget`,
  `PatternHeightMeasuredMoveTarget`) read every subscript UNCONDITIONALLY and
  freeze only the derived level inside `if BarsSinceEntry = 0`. That keeps the
  "reaches back" column honest under EL's non-short-circuiting evaluation, and
  it keeps `Highest`/`Lowest`/`Average` out of a conditionally-executed branch.
- Three catalog rows were checked as near matches and cleared, each named in
  its own row: `HighestHighBreakout` (vs `ExitAtPriorRangeExtreme`),
  `ConsecutiveCloseExit` (vs `ExitOnFirstOppositeBodyAfterRun`),
  `ProfitProtector` (vs `FailedTargetReversalExit`), plus `AtrProfitTarget`
  (vs `PatternHeightMeasuredMoveTarget`) and the slice-06 Entry
  `WeakFollowThroughBarRequiresPullback` (vs `ExitOnWeakFollowThrough`).
