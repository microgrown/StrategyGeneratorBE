## Not written

- `TrendBarBody` — **suspected duplicate of `StrongTrendBar` (slice 08, already on disk) at a fixed parameter, and the merge pass could not have seen it.** `StrongTrendBar` is `Close[lag] > Open[lag] AND Close[lag] − Open[lag] >= bodyFraction × range AND both tails <= tailFraction × range`. Given the body test, each tail is already at most `(1 − bodyFraction) × range`, so at any `tailFraction >= 1 − bodyFraction` (e.g. `tailFraction = 1`) the two tail terms are vacuously true and `StrongTrendBar` at `lag = 0` is `TrendBarBody` term for term, on both sides. Its `lag` input also covers the lag-1 uses. This is the most-cited primitive in the trilogy (16 sources; book body fractions 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.7, and the glossary's no-size-test form is it at `bodyFraction = 0`), so if Brian wants it as its own atomic rule the precedent exists — `StrongTrendBarRun`'s row records the same relation to `ConsecutiveUpBars` and both were kept. **Orchestrator decision needed**; nothing else in this slice depends on the file, because every rule that needs the trend-bar test inlines it.
- `MicroGapAroundTrendBar` — duplicate of `MicroMeasuringGap` (already on disk, slice 07/08). That rule is `Close[1] > Open[1] AND Close[1] − Open[1] >= bodyFraction × (High[1] − Low[1]) AND Low >= High[2]`, mirrored — this rule exactly, at its own `toleranceTicks` default of 0, and its row already records dropping the same tick tolerance. Only the `bodyFraction` default differs (0.7 there, 0.6 here); worth aligning to 0.6 in the grid pass if the eleven-source modal reading is wanted.
- `TrendBarCountInWindow` — duplicate of `StrongBodyBarCountInWindow` (slice 04). It was not on disk when the slice was checked and landed mid-pass; both twins were written, verified and then **deleted**, leaving slice 04's rule as the survivor. They are the same count: `CountIF(Close > Open AND Close − Open >= f × (High − Low), N) >= K`, current-inclusive, three degrees of freedom, mirrored. Only names and defaults differ — slice 04 has `windowBars(6)`, `minCount(3)`, `strongBodyFraction(0.6)`; this slice's five sources put the modal reading at 6 of 10, so **the grid pass should carry `windowBars 10` / `minCount 6` as a design point**. This also settles slice 11's held-back `StrongTrendBarCount`: same rule, drop it. At `minCount = windowBars` the rule degenerates to `ConsecutiveTrendBars` / `StrongTrendBarRun`, which is why those stay separate.

## Divergences and conventions

- **Tick units.** `ShavedTopBar`'s `shaveTicks(1)` became `shaveOffsetPoints(0.25)` and `ShavedBothEndsBar`'s `tailToleranceTicks(1)` became `tailOffsetPoints(0.25)` — `minmove` and `pricescale` are UNKNOWN and `ctx` exposes no tick size, so the defaults are the book's tick counts at the Emini's 0.25 points and are instrument-specific. `MicroGapAroundTrendBar`'s `toleranceTicks(0)` needed no substitute (the rule was not written). No other rule in the slice reached for ticks.
- **`maxlist` / `minlist` deliberately avoided.** `LargestBearBodyVsAverageBullBody`'s merged pseudocode clamps with `maxList(open − close, 0)`. The register ACCEPTS `MaxList` only for selection among tick-grid PRICE operands, and a body is a computed difference against a literal 0, so the clamp is written as an `If ... Then` over a running zero in both twins — the same substitution `BarOverlapFraction` (slice 01) makes.
- **A division removed on purpose.** The same rule's "largest body < K × AVERAGE body" is written as `max × lookback < K × sum` rather than dividing the sum by `lookback`. Both twins evaluate the identical product form, so they cannot disagree; the alternative risks EL's runtime divide-by-zero on a degenerate `lookback` and buys nothing.
- **`WFSafe_AvgTrueRange`** in `LargeTrendBarCountInWindow` and `ClimaxBarAfterTrendRunExit` is the rolling, two-statement, re-seeding accumulator from `AtrProfitTarget` / `ClimaxBar`, kept in `preConditionHook` so it advances on every bar as EasyLanguage's non-short-circuiting evaluation does. Both rules read it ONCE for the current bar and hold that value fixed across the counted window, rather than nesting a function-history reference inside the count.
- **Counts as EL numeric variables**, kept as `double` in C++, thresholds compared with `el_ge` — the `TailBarCountDominance` arrangement. The counted conditions are on PRICES, which have real history to the chart start, so no zero-filled variable mirror is needed anywhere in the slice.
- **Products, never quotients**, for every body- and range-fraction test in the slice: EL raises a runtime divide-by-zero and a zero-range bar is legal data.
- Every scratch value in a hook is a declared local rather than a hook-scope `double`, except the block-scoped temporaries inside the `WFSafe_` accumulator's own braces, which follow `ClimaxBar` exactly.
- No rule in the slice needed an unregistered EasyLanguage word, and none leans on an `ASSUMED` register row. EasyLanguage words used across the slice: `countif`, `absvalue`, `wfsafe_avgtruerange`, `marketposition`, `barssinceentry`, `openpositionprofit` — all VERIFIED or ACCEPTED. The merged pseudocode's `average` and `highest` for `LargestBearBodyVsAverageBullBody` were replaced by one explicit loop, because the clamped per-bar expressions they range over are not a price series either function can be handed.
