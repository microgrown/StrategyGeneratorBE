## Not written

- `PriceInLowerHalfOfChannel` — suspected duplicate the merge pass missed, of `EntryRoomToChannelLine` in this same slice. Algebraically the same rule: with `H = channelLine - trendLine`, `EntryRoomToChannelLine`'s long side is `channelLine - close >= minRoomFraction × H`, i.e. `close <= trendLine + (1 - minRoomFraction) × H`, which is `PriceInLowerHalfOfChannel`'s long side at `fraction = 1 - minRoomFraction`; the short sides coincide under the same substitution. At the merged defaults (`minRoomFraction` 0.5, `fraction` 0.5) they are not merely reparameterizations of each other but the SAME test, firing on identical bars. The merge notes distinguish them by intent ("trade viability" versus "position in the channel for direction"), which is not a behavioural difference. `EntryRoomToChannelLine` was authored and this one was not; if Brian wants the "band" reading (lower third, upper third) it is that rule at `minRoomFraction` 0.667. The horizontal proxy the miner offered alongside it (close below the Donchian midline) remains `AtrBandBreakout` at `atrMultiple = 0`, flipped, as `catalog_match` says.
- `StairsPattern` — blocked on `swinghigh` / `swinglow` **Occurrence 3+**, which the VERIFIED register rows list explicitly as unmeasured ("Unmeasured: `Length` below `Strength+1`, `Length = 0`, and Occurrence 3+"). The rule at its default `minStairs = 3` compares `swingLow(k)` against `swingLow(k+1)` and `swingHigh(k)` against `swingHigh(k+1)` for k in 1..2, so it needs occurrences 1, 2 AND 3 of both series; only 1 and 2 have been measured. Nothing else in the rule is a problem: the higher-high / higher-low / overlap test is ordinary arithmetic. Two ways forward, neither taken here: a probe extending `EL_Swing_Probe.txt` to Occurrence 3 and 4 unblocks it as written, or the two-pivot form (`swingLow(1) > swingLow(2) and swingHigh(1) > swingHigh(2) and swingLow(1) < swingHigh(2)`, the atomic stair step with its overlap) is authorable today but is a different rule from the one T26-01 defines and should not carry this key.
- `ShrinkingStairs` — blocked on the same thing, harder: its default `shrinkLegs = 3` compares three successive gaps between FOUR swing highs, so it needs Occurrences 1 through 4. Same probe unblocks it. Gated by `StairsPattern`, which is also blocked, so the pair should be revisited together.

## Notes for the orchestrator

- **The channel construction is a decision this slice had to make and every rule in it now shares.** `design_decisions_needed` said "trend channel line construction" for nine of the fourteen and the B-list addendum settles only the anchors (the two most recent CONFIRMED pivots at `strength`, older passed first, bars-ago convention), not which pivots each line runs through. Settled here: the BULL channel's trend line runs through the two most recent confirmed swing LOWS and its channel line through the two most recent confirmed swing HIGHS; the BEAR channel is the mirror, trend line across the HIGHS and channel line across the LOWS. The long side of every rule reads the bull channel and the short side the bear one. **The two lines are deliberately NOT parallel**: the classical "parallel dragged to the far side" construction gives a constant width, under which `ChannelWidthConvergence` could never fire, and Brooks draws the across-two-pushes form throughout. B02's `TrendChannelLineOvershoot` and `TrendChannelLineTarget` are described in `B_KEYS.md` as the PARALLEL form; if those slices author the parallel, the corpus will hold two different channel lines and the rows should say so.
- Every rule needs a `pivotLookback`, which the merged `parameters` omitted (only `pivotStrength` was given). Default 20, matching `rules/SwingHigh.json`. It is load-bearing: it is EL's `Length`, so it bounds how old the anchors may be, and in `ChannelStartRetestEntry` it is what decides how far back "the start of the channel" is.
- Nothing in the slice needed an unregistered word and nothing leans on an `ASSUMED` row. Words used: `swinghigh`, `swinglow`, `swinghighbar`, `swinglowbar`, `tlvalue`, `wfsafe_avgtruerange`, `high`, `low`, `open`, `close`, all VERIFIED. `countif` was in `SecondChannelLinePenetration`'s `el_words` and is deliberately NOT used — see its row.
- The `-1` sentinel is guarded in all eleven, and every `TLValue` call is behind a `B1 <> B2` guard, per the register's instruction not to rely on the measured 0.0 return. In the C++ twins the pivot slope is hoisted once per bar out of the per-target arithmetic where EL recomputes it inside each `TLValue` call; the inputs are identical doubles, so it is the same line bit for bit.
- Outside the EL translation table: nothing. No `classMembersHook`, no `std::` containers, no ternaries in the conditions; the only C++-only constructs are loop-local `int`/`bool` scratch inside the hooks, as in `TightChannel` and `BreakoutLevelRetest`.
- `pivotStrength` and `pivotLookback` should be PINNED in grids, not optimized: they change which pivots exist and so change every line in the rule discontinuously. `bodyFraction`, the ATR multiples and the window lengths are the tunable ones.
