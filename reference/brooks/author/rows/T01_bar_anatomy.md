## Existing rule amended

`MicroChannelRunLength` — the orchestrator decision of 2026-09-17 (T02's `HigherLowStreak` is this rule plus a tick tolerance). `minTicks(1)` added to BOTH twins; nothing else changed. **What `minTicks` does:** it is how deep a pullback has to be to BREAK the run. A bar breaks the bull channel only if its low is at least `minTicks` ticks below the prior bar's low, so a lower low of up to `minTicks − 1` ticks is forgiven (mirrored on highs for the short side). The break test becomes `Low[kk] < Low[kk + 1] − (minTicks − 1) * tickSize`. **Both requirements hold at the default, with nothing traded off:** at `minTicks = 1` the subtracted term is `(1 − 1) * tickSize = 0`, so the comparison is character for character the original strict one — existing behaviour and every existing template are unchanged — and it is simultaneously `HigherLowStreak` at ITS default, whose merged alive-condition is `low[i] ≥ low[i+1] − (minTicks − 1) * tickSize` over `noPullbackBars = minBars` pairs. Full replacement CATALOG row (swap in for line 154):

| `MicroChannelRunLength` | Entry | mirrored | `minBars` | `minBars(5)`, `minTicks(1)` | None of the last `minBars` bar pairs broke the channel — no bar traded `minTicks` or more ticks below the prior bar's low (long) / above its high (short) — Brooks's micro channel. A run counted back from the current bar reaches `minBars` exactly when the bounded all-pairs test holds, so the counter needs no persisted state.
