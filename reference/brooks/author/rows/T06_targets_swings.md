# T06 targets + swing structure (tick-unit list, 2026-09-17)

CATALOG rows for the ten rules of `slicesT/T06_targets_swings.json`. All ten were
written; both twins exist for each.

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |

| `LegOneEqualsLegTwoTarget` | Exit | mirrored | `lookback - 1 + pivotStrength` | `pivotStrength(2)`, `lookback(40)`, `multiple(1)`, `toleranceTicks(1)` | Price reaches the leg-1 = leg-2 measured move, `D = C + multiple × (B − A)`: for a long, `A` is swing low occurrence 2, `B` swing high occurrence 1, `C` swing low occurrence 1, and the long exits when the high reaches `D − toleranceTicks × tick`; the short mirror runs `A` = swing high occurrence 2, `B` = swing low occurrence 1, `C` = swing high occurrence 1, and the SAME formula serves it because `B − A` is negative there. ONLY OCCURRENCES 1 AND 2 are read — occurrence 3+ is unmeasured — and the ORDERING GUARD is this rule's own construction, not the merged pseudocode's: the bars-ago counts must descend `loBar2 > hiBar1 > loBar1` and the first leg must actually be up (`hiPx1 > loPx2`), or an interleaved pivot pair would project off two unrelated legs. Not `PatternHeightMeasuredMoveTarget` (height = the breakout window's own span, projected off the extreme that broke; here the height is the PRIOR leg and the projection starts at the PULLBACK extreme), not `GapMidpointMeasuredMoveTarget` (a midpoint projection), not `SwingPivotTarget` (exits AT a pivot, projects nothing) |
| `RollingRangeMidpointTarget` | Exit | mirrored | `max(rangeBars, legLookback) - 1` | `rangeBars(15)`, `legLookback(20)`, `toleranceTicks(1)` | Price reaches `2 × mid − legStart`, where `mid` is the midpoint of the last `rangeBars` bars RE-ESTIMATED EVERY BAR (Brooks's "keep adjusting the line" while the pullback range is still forming) and `legStart` is frozen on the fill bar as the lowest low (long) / highest high (short) of `legLookback` bars. The candidate's own "measured move up equal to the open-to-low distance projected from the day's low" collapses to exactly the session open, so the mechanized rule is one price held across the session. Session model per the standing decision: `date > date[1]`, mirrored as `day_of(ctx.Time(0)) > day_of(ctx.Time(1))`, the open kept in a local and reset there — `OpenD(0)` is VERIFIED but has no engine counterpart, so both twins carry it in a variable and stay structurally identical (`SessionMoveExhaustsDailyRange`'s choice). `haveSess` is the warm-up guard: before the run's first date change the variable still holds EL's initial 0, which as a price sits under everything. Written for a long entered BELOW the open, so a long entered above it exits on its first bar — a placement concern, the same trap `MovingAverageTarget` carries. Intraday only. | `MovingAverageTarget` | Exit | mirrored | 0 (the EMA is recursive and no history is read) | `emaLength(20)`, `toleranceTicks(1)` | The moving average is the minimum target of a countertrend scalp: the long exits when the bar's HIGH reaches `ema − toleranceTicks × tick`, the short when the low reaches `ema +` it. | `SwingPointUndercut` | Entry | mirrored | `maxBarsBack - 1 + strength` | `strength(2)`, `offsetTicks(0)`, `maxBarsBack(60)` | The bar's low trades at or below the most recent CONFIRMED swing low by at least `offsetTicks`; short mirrored at the swing high. Eleven candidates, one behaviour: seven differ only in the CONTEXT GATE they attach (range bottom, bull channel, bear channel, pullback in a bull trend, late-stage channel), each of which is a rule in another bucket that the pane ANDs; two are the NEGATION, which is the per-placement toggle; the scale-in sizing two attach is not expressible and is dropped. `offsetTicks` defaults to 0 rather than Brooks's one tick below, per the standing limit-fill convention (signal-bar close, fill at the next bar's open). Kept apart from `DoubleExtremeBreakout` because the level is different and much further away. ## Notes for the roll-up

- **`DoubleExtreme` does not exist on disk** and is in no slice, so the three
  `DoubleExtreme*` rules INLINE the pattern, all three spelling it identically:
  the two most recent CONFIRMED swing pivots on one side are within `matchTicks`
  of each other and the more recent of them confirmed no more than
  `patternWindow` bars ago. **`matchTicks` and `pivotLookback` are promoted
  inputs** — the merged parameters name neither the tolerance that makes two
  extremes "the same price" nor the window the pivots are sought in, and without
  the first there is no double anything. **The pattern's LEVEL is the LOWER of
  the two lows** (the higher of the two highs for a double top): it is the price
  a breakout has to clear, and choosing it this way is what lets the pullback,
  the breakout and the dip rule all read one level. If `DoubleExtreme` is ever
  authored, it should be this test, and these three should keep their inlined
  copies per the composite decision.
- Distinct from **`MicroDoubleExtreme`** (slice T03), which matches two BAR
  extremes at a fixed small lag (`gap`, default 1) with a 1-tick tolerance: that
  is a two-bar micro form anchored at a fixed offset, where these three match two
  CONFIRMED PIVOTS any distance apart inside `pivotLookback`. Distinct from
  `Ledge`, which counts bars matching the CURRENT bar's extreme inside a window.
- **`SwingPivotTarget`** (on disk) exits AT a pivot with an ATR tolerance and no
  other leg; `SwingHighProfitTarget` adds the dollar gate and a SIGNED tick
  offset, and `LegOneEqualsLegTwoTarget` projects off three anchors rather than
  reading one.
- `SwingPointUndercut`, `CloseBeyondLastSwingPoint`, `SwingHighProfitTarget`,
  `LegOneEqualsLegTwoTarget` and the three `DoubleExtreme*` rules all inline the
  measured `SwingHigh` scan (at or beyond the `strength` OLDER bars, strictly
  beyond the `strength` NEWER ones, lag exactly `strength`, `-1` sentinel
  guarded, `el_gt`/`el_ge`). `CloseBeyondLastSwingPoint` hand-rolls the scan in
  the EL twin as well, since its count can exceed occurrence 2; the rest use the
  `SwingHigh`/`SwingLow`/`SwingHighBar`/`SwingLowBar` built-ins at occurrences 1
  and 2 only.
- Every tick margin is written division-first in both twins —
  `ticks * (MinMove / PriceScale)` in EL, `ticks * (ctx.MinMove() /
  ctx.PriceScale())` in C++ — computed once per bar into a local `tickSz`, per
  the tick-unit addendum. No `ticks` default was rescaled.
- Sentinel and count tests are assigned to `bool` locals in the hook and the
  conditions read the flag (`haveLo`, `haveDb`, `longSig`, …), as
  `SwingPivotTarget` does: a bare `!= -1.0` or `>= needN` in a condition trips
  `lintElFeatures`'s bare-relational warning.
