# T02_bar_offsets — CATALOG rows

Slice `reference/brooks/author/slicesT/T02_bar_offsets.json` (12 keys). Eight rules
written, four not. All eight are stated in ticks, converted at the comparison with
the division-first `ticks * (MinMove / PriceScale)` form (`ctx.MinMove() / ctx.PriceScale()`
in C++), computed once per bar into a `tickSize` local in both twins; `minmove` and
`pricescale` are VERIFIED. Every scratch value is a `localVariables` entry, so the
hooks survive a double placement.

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BarLowHoldsPriorClose` | Entry | mirrored | 1 | `toleranceTicks(1)` | The bar's low never fell more than N ticks below the PRIOR bar's close (short: its high never rose that far above it) — near-zero give-back inside a spike. ONE-SIDED, not a band: R02-07's and T19-04's extra `low ≤ close[1]` term would exclude the strongest bars, the ones that never trade back into the prior bar at all, so V00-45's plain form wins. Absorbs the merged key `LowNearPriorClose`, which is this identical expression with the input spelled `overlapTicks` and the same default of 1 (V00-37, T19-04). Not `CloseNearPriorLow`, which reads this bar's CLOSE against the prior bar's LOW plus an offset in price points |
| `HigherHighByTicks` | Entry | mirrored | 1 | `extensionTicks(4)` | This bar's high is at least N ticks above the prior bar's high (short: its low that far below the prior low) — V15-08's follow-through bar extending "many ticks", not "a few". The margin IS the rule; `LaggedHigherHigh` has no margin input and at `extensionTicks = 1` the two nearly coincide on a tick grid. Brooks gives no number, so 4 is the miner's reading and the input to optimize. Reads only highs, so it is knowable before the bar closes. Distinct from `BreakoutClearsPriorExtremeByMargin` (a whole window, not one bar) except at that rule's `lookback = 1`, where the two coincide exactly |
| `BreakoutClearsPriorExtremeByMargin` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback` | `lookback(20)`, `marginTicks(4)` | The bar's high clears `Highest(High, lookback)[1]` — the highest high of the N bars ENDING ONE BAR AGO — by at least M ticks; short mirrored on `Lowest(Low, N)[1]`. R02-06's 4 ticks past a swing high and V07-11's 3 ticks past a flag boundary are the same margin test, default 4; the fixed window replaces the swing search per the merge (least machinery, the construction `CloseAbovePriorHighestHigh` already uses). Not `CloseAbovePriorHighestHigh`, which reads the CLOSE against the same level with no margin — the two disagree on every bar that pokes through and closes back under, exactly the case Brooks separates. The C++ writes the window out as the `Extremes` loop it bottoms out in; only the value is read, so the tie rule cannot show |
| `CountertrendNeedsPriorPush` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback + 1` | `lookback(5)`, `minPushTicks(2)` | At least ONE of the N bars BEFORE this one pushed its high more than K ticks above the high of the bar before it (short: its low that far below the prior low) — T10-07's permission to take a countertrend trade, evidence the side you are joining can move price at all. The cheap substitute for `CountertrendNeedsPriorTrendLineBreak`: no pivots, no line. An existence test over bars 1..N written as an explicit loop in both twins, NOT `CountIF`, which counts bars 0..N−1 including the current bar — the wrong window, since the evidence must pre-date the signal. Strict ("more than"). Not `LaggedHigherHigh`, which tests one specific bar with no margin |
| `BreakoutFollowThroughTicks` | Entry | mirrored | `confirmBars + swingLookback - 1` | `swingLookback(20)`, `confirmBars(3)`, `scalpTicks(4)` | The best high of the last `confirmBars` bars is at least `scalpTicks` above the highest high of the `swingLookback` bars BEFORE them (short mirrored on lows) — R04-01's test of whether a new extreme found enough buyers for the breakout traders to scalp a profit; a rolling trend-strength gauge, re-evaluated every bar. The candidate's swing high is re-expressed over `Highest`/`Lowest` windows (no pivot detection, the cheaper definition per MERGE). BOTH windows are written out as the `Extremes` scan in both twins, because the older one is `Highest(High, swingLookback)[confirmBars]` — a history reference at an INPUT offset, the case `FailedBreakoutReversal` open-codes; seeding each loop at its window's first bar also keeps the EL `for` from ever running backwards for inputs ≥ 1. Brooks's other half — a new extreme that reverses within a bar or two — is the per-placement negate toggle, not a second rule. Not `CloseAbovePriorHighestHigh` (the close, one window, no margin) |
| `DipBelowPriorBarLow` | Entry | mirrored | 1 | `dipTicks(1)` | Price traded at least N ticks below the prior bar's low (short: that far above the prior high) — the resting with-trend limit order bulls keep at or below the prior bar's low through a bull channel, and the same order that fades a countertrend setup bar. Absorbs the merged key `DipBelowPriorBarLowByTicks`, the identical expression with the input spelled `offsetTicks`. Seven book statements, one parameter; offsets seen 0 (T04-11, T06-30, T21-16, R32-03), 1–3 (T15-18 p273) and **4** (R28-25, a buy 4 ticks under the bear signal bar's low, i.e. 2–3 ticks above where the short scalpers exit) — default 1, the most supported reading, with 4 the other book default to test. Non-strict, so at `dipTicks = 0` it is `LaggedHigherHigh(0)` flipped only up to a tie, and the default separates them. The sources' channel/strong-trend context is a separate placement (`TightChannel`, the always-in read); the entry pane ANDs |
| `ShallowDipBelowPriorBarLow` | Entry | mirrored | 1 | `dipTicks(4)` | The bar dips below the prior bar's low but by NO MORE than N ticks (short: pokes above the prior high by no more than N) — T16-05 and R28-18's limit order sized from the micro channel's own largest pullback ("if the biggest pullback has been five ticks, buy any three- or four-tick pullback"). A BAND, so it is neither `DipBelowPriorBarLow` at a parameter nor that rule negated: that one asks for AT LEAST N ticks of dip and deepens as its input rises, this one asks for AT MOST N and loosens as its input rises, and the two fire on disjoint sets whenever both bounds bite. Lower edge strict, upper edge not. Default 4 (T16-05); R28-18 gives no number |
| `DipBelowPriorLowCloseBackAbove` | Entry | mirrored | 1 | `dipTicks(3)` | The bar dips below the prior bar's low by no more than N ticks AND closes back above that low (short mirrored on the high) — the shallow pullback that was REJECTED. TP3-17's bounded form ("the first pullback usually only falls below the low of the prior bar by a few ticks", 3) merged with T07-23's unbounded "aggressive bulls buy below the low of the prior bar"; a large `dipTicks` disables the bound. Kept whole, like a crossing: dipped-below plus closed-back-above is one event and neither half is the claim. Drop the close term and it is exactly `ShallowDipBelowPriorBarLow`; drop the bound and it is `DipBelowPriorBarLow`; no rule in the corpus tests the close against the prior bar's low from above, so no pair of placements reaches it. **Merged key was `ShallowDipBelowPriorLow`, renamed** — one word apart from `ShallowDipBelowPriorBarLow`, a real and different rule in the same slice. Limit fills mechanized at the close, filling at the next open, per the standing convention |

## Not written

- `LowNearPriorClose` — **duplicate of `BarLowHoldsPriorClose`** (authored). Deciding test: *would it fire on the same bars?* Both are `low[0] ≥ close[1] − N·tick` mirrored on the high, both default N = 1; only the input's name differs (`overlapTicks` vs `toleranceTicks`). Its sources (V00-37, T19-04) and the dropped `low ≤ close[1]` term are recorded in the surviving rule's row and comment. Not named in the addendum's cluster, but the same three tests apply.
- `DipBelowPriorBarLowByTicks` — **duplicate of `DipBelowPriorBarLow`** (authored). Deciding test: *would it fire on the same bars?* Both are `low[0] ≤ low[1] − N·tick` mirrored on the high; only the input's name (`offsetTicks` vs `dipTicks`) and the default differ. Both books' defaults are carried on the surviving row: 1 (T15-18, T04-11, T06-30, T21-16, R32-03) and 4 (R28-25).
- `ShallowDipBelowPriorBarLow` vs `DipBelowPriorBarLow` — **both written, not duplicates.** "Dips at least N ticks below" and "dips no more than N ticks below" are two different rules: neither is the other at a fixed parameter (they move in opposite directions as N grows), and neither is the other negated (negating the at-least form admits bars that never dipped at all).
- `CloseWithinTicksOfHigh` — **duplicate of T01's `CloseNearBarExtreme`; deferred to T01**, whose key stays in `T_KEYS.md`. Deciding test: *would it fire on the same bars?* `close[0] ≥ high[0] − N·tick` and `(high[0] − close[0]) ≤ N·tick` are the same inequality rearranged, same mirrored short side, same default of 1 tick. Nothing here differs, so T02 writes nothing. Its extra source (R02-09, the shaved close counted over a spike window) and its `CloseRangePosition` distinction — an ABSOLUTE tick tolerance, not a fraction of the bar's range — belong on T01's row.
- `HigherLowStreak` — **suspected duplicate of the existing `MicroChannelRunLength`, stopped for a decision** (skill §2 / AUTHORING per-rule gate 1; the merge's `catalog_match` named `LaggedHigherHigh` and missed this one). Deciding test: *is it an existing rule at a fixed parameter?* At its own default `minTicks = 1` the tolerance is zero and the condition is `low[i] ≥ low[i+1]` for every pair in the window — bar for bar `MicroChannelRunLength(minBars = noPullbackBars)`, whose containment is likewise INCLUSIVE, with the same both-sides mirror. The only thing `HigherLowStreak` adds is the tick tolerance (`minTicks ≥ 2` forgives a shallow lower low), which is a new INPUT on an existing rule rather than a new rule — the `VolumeBelowAverage` / `volumeFraction` precedent, and the reason slice 13's `HigherLowBar` was removed. Editing an existing rule is outside an authoring agent's remit, so: **Brian's call** — add `minTicks` to `MicroChannelRunLength` (defaulting 1, every existing template unchanged), or drop the key. Sources R00-55 (no pullback bar within 5 bars) and V09-02 (`low[0] > low[1]`, strict, the single-bar form).
