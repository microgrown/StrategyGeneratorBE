None — all seven keys in the slice were authored. Three were checked hard against the on-disk three-push family and kept, with the relationship stated in each rule's comment and in the row above:

## Notes for the orchestrator

- **Construction reused, not reinvented.** All four pattern rules inline `ThreePushPattern`'s pivot scan verbatim (measured mixed test, lag exactly `swingStrength`, candidate offsets `0..lookbackBars-1`, `-1` sentinel guarded, `el_gt`/`el_ge`/`el_lt`/`el_le`, written without `Break` so the twins are statement for statement, since occurrence 3 is UNMEASURED). `StopRunReclaimReentry` needs occurrence 1 only, so its EL twin calls the measured `SwingLow`/`SwingHigh` built-ins while the C++ inlines the scan, the split `TrendLineBreak` uses. The neckline is `TLValue`'s measured first-anchor form with the older anchor first and `B1 <> B2` guarded. The two session rules use the standing date-change session model.
- **Decisions made here that the brief did not settle:** the pivot COUNT as the mechanization of "alternating legs" in `ExpandingTrianglePattern`, and its strict-plus-margin pair so the default `minExpansionTicks = 0` still means "above"; the date-change (one-bar-late) reading of the session end in `SessionCloseStrengthBias` instead of the merged note's clock-time input; the neckline anchored on low occurrences 3 and 2 for a top (occurrence 1 is the low being broken); `lookbackBars` and `shoulderSymmetryTicks` promoted as inputs in `HeadAndShouldersNecklineBreakout` because the pattern is inlined.
- **Ticks** are computed once per bar into `tickSize` in both twins, C++ `ctx.MinMove() / ctx.PriceScale()`, EL `MinMove / PriceScale`, division-first, with every margin written `ticks * tickSize` at the comparison. No `ticks` input was rescaled into points or ATR fractions.
- **Feed and timeframe caveats:** `PremarketExtremeTest` requires a feed carrying overnight/Globex bars and is silent without one; both session rules are intraday (`SessionCloseStrengthBias` degenerates rather than breaking on a daily series).
- All seven pass `validateRule` with no errors and no warnings, and `python lintElFeatures.py <Key>` reports 0 blocking / 0 warnings for each. No engine build and no `compileCheck.py` was run, per the slice brief.
