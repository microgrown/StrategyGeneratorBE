# CATALOG rows — slice 01, bar anatomy (inside / outside / overlap)

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BarOverlapFraction` | Entry | same | `1` | `overlapThreshold(0.5)` | This bar's range overlaps at least fraction T of the PRIOR bar's range: `ov = MinList(High, High[1]) − MaxList(Low, Low[1])`, clamped to 0 on a gap, and `ov >= T × (High[1] − Low[1])`. Brooks's one-bar trading-range clue; place negated for the spike (little-overlap) sense. Written as a multiplication so a zero-range prior bar cannot divide by zero. The gap clamp is an EL `If ov < 0 Then ov = 0`, not `MaxList(0, ov)` — 0 against a price DIFFERENCE is a computed operand and outside MinList/MaxList's ACCEPTED scope. Not `TwoBarRangePosition` (that places the close in a two-bar range) |
| `BarOverlapIncreasing` | Entry | same | `lagBars + 1` | `lagBars(4)`, `epsilon(0.000001)` | The current bar's overlap with its predecessor, as a fraction of that predecessor's range, is greater than the same reading taken `lagBars` bars ago. A CHANGE in overlap, not a level, so no threshold on `BarOverlapFraction` reaches it — Brooks's stage-3 sign that a trend is deteriorating into a trading range. The ε guards a zero-range reference bar (EL raises a runtime divide-by-zero on a zero denominator) |
| `BarOverlapWithPriorRange` | Entry | same | `lookback` | `lookback(3)`, `overlapFraction(0.3)` | This bar overlaps the COMBINED high-low block of the prior N bars by at most fraction F of that block: `ph = Highest(High, lookback)[1]`, `pl = Lowest(Low, lookback)[1]`, `MinList(High, ph) − MaxList(Low, pl) <= F × (ph − pl)`. Brooks's signal-bar quality test, "not much overlap with the prior bar or bars". Measured against a block, not a single neighbour, so it stays low even when the bar overlaps one neighbour heavily |
| `ConsecutiveInsideBars` | Entry | same | `lag + insideBarCount` | `insideBarCount(2)`, `lag(0)` | N consecutive INCLUSIVE inside relations ending at the bar `lag` bars ago — `insideBarCount(2)` is Brooks's ii, `(3)` is iii. Containment is inclusive (Brooks p137, "of the same size or smaller"), so the failure tests are strict and a bar tying the prior high or low still counts as inside. That boundary is the only difference from `InsideBar`, which is strict; at `insideBarCount(1)` this is `InsideBarInclusive` |
| `InsideBarInclusive` | Entry | same | `lag + 1` | `lag(0)` | The bar N bars ago is inside the bar before it on Brooks's INCLUSIVE boundary: `High[lag] <= High[lag+1] AND Low[lag] >= Low[lag+1]`. Same lag convention as `InsideBar`, which is the STRICT form — the two differ on every bar that ties the prior high or low, which on tick-grid futures bars is common. Default lag is 0 (this bar), not `InsideBar`'s 1 |
| `InsideBodyBar` | Entry | same | `lag + 1` | `lag(0)` | The bar's BODY nests inside the prior bar's body — `MaxList(Open, Close) <= MaxList(Open[1], Close[1])` and `MinList(Open, Close) >= MinList(Open[1], Close[1])` at offset `lag` — even though its tails may extend beyond the prior bar's range. Brooks's explicitly weaker bodies-only ii/iii variant; fires on bars `InsideBar` cannot, and vice versa |
| `IoiPattern` | Entry | same | `lag + 3` | `lag(0)` | Inside bar, then an outside bar, then an inside bar over three consecutive bars ending at the bar `lag` bars ago — Brooks's literal i-o-i breakout setup, all three relations tested inclusively. Direction comes from the breakout, hence identical both sides. The "close beyond the inside bar" term is left to the pane (`CloseAbovePriorHigh`) |
| `LargeOrCentredIoiInsideBar` | Entry | same | `4` | `maxInsideFraction(0.5)`, `centreBand(0.15)` | An ioi completed one bar ago (inside at [3], outside at [2], inside at [1]) whose final inside bar is either large relative to the outside bar, `High[1] − Low[1] >= F × (High[2] − Low[2])`, OR sits near the outside bar's centre, `AbsValue(mid[1] − mid[2]) <= C × (High[2] − Low[2])`. Positive composite: place NEGATED for Brooks's veto — skip the ioi breakout when the second inside bar is too big (you buy the top of a range) or is barbwire. An OR inside an AND, so it cannot be split across panes |
| `LargeOutsideBar` | Entry | same | `lag + rangeLookback` | `lag(1)`, `largeMultiple(1.5)`, `rangeLookback(10)` | The bar `lag` bars ago is an outside bar AND its range is at least M × `Average(High − Low, rangeLookback)[lag+1]` — the mean bar range over the window ending just before it, so the bar is not in its own benchmark. Positive composite: place NEGATED for Brooks's veto (never enter on a large outside bar's breakout; the protective stop sits too far away). The pane ANDs, so `NOT(outside AND large)` is not reachable from the two parts separately. Not `BarRangeAboveStd`, which uses a mean-plus-K-sigma band over a mirrored variable history and does not test the outside shape |
| `NarrowRangeBar` | Entry | same | `rangeLookback - 1` | `rangeFraction(0.5)`, `rangeLookback(20)` | This bar's high−low range is at most F × `Average(High − Low, rangeLookback)`, the window INCLUSIVE of the current bar as the source writes it. Brooks's small pause bar sized against recent bars. Plain mean of the high−low range, so a fresh `Average` over a price expression — not `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange`, and not the zero-filled variable history `BarRangeAboveStd` mirrors. Neither the numerator nor the denominator of `BodyBelowAtrFraction` (body vs ATR); the opposite sense of `BarRangeAboveStd` but a different statistic |
| `OioPattern` | Entry | same | `lag + 3` | `lag(0)` | Outside bar, then an inside bar, then an outside bar over three consecutive bars ending at the bar `lag` bars ago, all relations inclusive. Direction comes from the breakout, hence identical both sides; the source's "bull body on the last bar" term is `ConsecutiveUpBars(1)` ANDed in the pane and is not carried here |
| `OutsideBarInMidRange` | Entry | same | `max(lag + rangeBars - 1, lag + 1)` | `lag(1)`, `rangeBars(20)`, `lowerBand(0.33)`, `upperBand(0.67)`, `epsilon(0.000001)` | The bar `lag` bars ago is an outside bar AND its midpoint sits strictly inside the middle band of the M-bar high-low block ending at that bar: `pos = (mid − Lowest(Low, rangeBars)[lag]) / (Highest(High, rangeBars)[lag] − Lowest(Low, rangeBars)[lag] + ε)`, `lowerBand < pos < upperBand`. Positive composite: place NEGATED for Brooks's veto — an outside bar in the middle of a trading range is meaningless. Not `CloseRangePosition`, which places the close inside its OWN bar |

Both are the mean pairwise bar-overlap fraction over an N-bar window; slice 01 states the test as "at or above T" and slice 12 as "at or below T", and negation is a per-placement toggle, so the pair is redundant by construction (skill §2, "is it an existing rule negated or flipped?"). Slice 01's merge note itself says the sources read it as the low-overlap test and that it should be "placed negated", i.e. it is slice 12's rule. **Stopped for a decision**: keep one key (slice 12's `AverageBarOverlap` reads in the sources' own direction), or state a real difference. Note the merged forms also disagree on the divisor — slice 01 chose the PRIOR bar's range (T15-41), and slice 12's row does not say; if slice 12 divides by the current bar's range (TP3-27) that is the only distinction, and it is a weak one. Nothing was written for this key in either repo.

## Notes for the orchestrator

- `ThinAreaLowBarOverlap` (02), `OverlappingBarRun` (11) and `ConsecutiveBarsLittleOverlap` (07) are all-bars-in-window tests, genuinely distinct from `BarOverlapFraction`'s single pair and from the windowed MEAN above. `OverlappingBarRun` and `ThinAreaLowBarOverlap` do look like each other negated, but neither is mine.
- No `OutsideBar` key exists in `rules/` or in `A_KEYS.md`, though four rules in this slice list it in `depends_on_keys`. The outside-bar relation is inlined in each as the inclusive mirror of `InsideBarInclusive` (`High[k] >= High[k+1] AND Low[k] <= Low[k+1]`), which is the form R00-09, V00-07 and V00-09 all wrote. If an atomic `OutsideBar` is later authored it should use that same boundary. The inner "midpoint in the middle of the block" test of `OutsideBarInMidRange` would also stand alone as an atomic rule (flagged by the merge pass); it was not written, as it is not a key in this slice.
