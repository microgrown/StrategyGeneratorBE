# Slice 01_swing_channel: CATALOG rows

Three rules (`ChannelFailedSwingFromHitSide`, `ChannelInnerSwingBreakFade`, `InnerBoxFalseBreakReclaim`) inline the ON-1-a swing box verbatim. The box is built from an anchor pair of confirmed `swingStrength` pivots in either order (Brian 2026-11-05). Anchor A is a swing low that is also the lowest low of the `anchorLookback` bars ending at it, and B is the very next pivot in time, a swing high ("LH"). The mirror ordering is "HL". The box is live while no bar since A has traded beyond either side, and it is LATCHED: it is not replaced until it breaks, and on the bar it breaks the scan looks for the most recent qualifying pair inside `maxLookback`. Each dependent rule replays the pivots after its reference point on the bar the box latches, and after that processes only the pivot newly confirmed that bar. Every read therefore stays inside `maxLookback`, however long the box lives. A brute-force recomputation from scratch agreed on every bar of a 33k-bar synthetic run.

Two rules (`SupportBreakResistanceThenReclaim`, `RedRunThroughStackedSupportFade`) inline the I2 support zone. A zone is M consecutive non-alternating swing lows lying within W x ATR (max - min) whose population standard deviation is at most K x ATR. It is killed by a close more than a margin x ATR below its bottom. Members are re-scanned, because EL arrays have no register row.

All pivots use the measured `SwingHigh` test, hand-rolled as in `SwingPivotTarget`. A bar that is both a swing high and a swing low counts as both, ordered by colour: green is low-then-high, any other bar high-then-low (Brian 2026-11-05; same convention as slice 02). ATR is `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange` in the rolling form from `AtrProfitTarget`.
