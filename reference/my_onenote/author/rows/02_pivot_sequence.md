# Slice 02_pivot_sequence: CATALOG rows

All five rules inline one pivot-sequence form, copied verbatim: a newest-first merge of the confirmed swing highs and lows of `strength` in the window (non-alternating, the measured `SwingHigh` test hand-rolled as in `SwingPivotTarget`, because Occurrence 3+ is unmeasured), with the newest four kept as type / price / bars-ago slots and a total count. A bar that is both a swing high and a swing low enters twice, ordered by colour (Brian 2026-11-05). Rule-specific bookkeeping is added inside the push step only.

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FractalBoxBreakout` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback - 1 + strength` | `strength(2)`, `lookback(100)` | The four most recent confirmed swing pivots, oldest to newest, are low, high, low, high, with the last two inside the range of the first two (inclusive, `el_ge`/`el_le`): a contracting box whose edges are the last two pivots. The mirror order high, low, high, low with the same containment also counts (Brian 2026-11-05). Long on the FIRST close above the box since its newest pivot printed (no close on bars `1 .. pB1 - 1` outside the box), short on the first close below; it fires at most once per box. A newer pivot replaces the pattern, since the four must be the most recent. The p.60 fade is the per-placement flip. Pivots come from the shared non-alternating pivot sequence (both-type bars ordered by colour). Not `DoubleExtremeBreakout` or `TrendLineBreak`, which break a level built from two same-side pivots. Source: Brian's notes (ON-50, #50). |
| `LateFractalFade` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback - 1 + strength` | `strength(2)`, `lookback(300)`, `pivotCount(8)`, `lateMultiple(2)` | On the bar a new pivot is confirmed (newest pivot at bars-ago `strength`), the gap in bars since the pivot before it (either type) is at least `lateMultiple` times the mean gap between the `pivotCount` pivots before the new one (`pivotCount - 1` gaps). A late swing low is a long, a late swing high a short (a fade). "2-3x" is a minimum multiple; no cap. `lookback` must hold `pivotCount + 1` pivots, else silent; `pivotCount < 2` never fires. A bar that is both a swing high and a swing low gives a zero gap to its twin, so it is never late. Not `SwingLegLengthAtLeast`, which tests a fixed leg length, not relative lateness. Source: Brian's notes (ON-51, #51). |
| `FractalOrderLowHighHigh` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback - 1 + strength` | `strength(2)`, `lookback(100)` | The three most recent confirmed swing pivots, oldest to newest, are a swing low followed by two swing highs with no low between them (possible because swings are non-alternating): long. High, low, low: short. No price relation between the two highs (lows) is required. Fires once, on the bar the third pivot is confirmed (newest pivot at bars-ago `strength`). Both-type bars ordered by colour (Brian 2026-11-05). Not `HigherSwingLow` or `ThreePushPattern`, which compare pivot prices on one side; this reads only the type order of the merged sequence. Source: Brian's notes (ON-53, #53). |
| `FractalLegSlopeDecay` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback - 1 + strength` | `strength(2)`, `lookback(100)` | Short: the four most recent confirmed pivots, oldest to newest, are low L1, high H1, low L2, high H2, and the last up-leg L2→H2 rises less per bar than the first up-leg L1→H1 (momentum fading). Long mirrors: high, low, high, low with the last down-leg falling less per bar than the first. Slopes are price per bar, compared cross-multiplied with `el_lt` (both bar spans required positive), so the test is scale-free, shift-invariant and division-free. No price relation such as H2 > H1 is required. Fires once, on the bar the fourth pivot is confirmed. Not `ThreePushPattern` (push extents on one side) or `TrendLineSlopeFlattening` (trend-line slopes). Source: Brian's notes (ON-59, #59). |
| `FractalMovePullbackContraction` | Entry | mirrored | `pivotLookback - 1 + strength` | `strength(3)`, `Q(4)`, `R(0.5)`, `maxPullbackBars(15)`, `pivotLookback(150)` | Short as written: the latest swing low of `strength` (3 bars each side, Brian 2026-11-05) is the lowest of the last `Q` swing lows (ties allowed; fewer than `Q` lows in the window means no signal), and the move runs down to it from the latest swing high before it in the pivot sequence. The bars since that low are the pullback: between `strength` and `maxPullbackBars` of them, every bar's high and low inside the move's range, and their mean bar range (high − low) at most `R` times the move's mean bar range (the move's bars from its high's bar to its low's bar, inclusive; sums newest-first). Long mirrors: the latest swing high is the highest of the last `Q` swing highs, the move runs up to it from the latest swing low before it. Fires on every bar the conditions hold through the pullback, not once. Not `FirstPullbackAfterSpike` / `BreakoutPullbackEntry` (spike or breakout bar anchors) or `MaxPullbackDepthBelowThreshold` (depth only). Source: Brian's notes (P6, P6). |
