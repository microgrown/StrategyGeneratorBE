# Slice 07_moving_averages: CATALOG rows

Every EMA here is MultiWalk's `WFSafe_Xaverage` in the measured `EmaGapBar` form: the price seed on bar 1, `x + sf*(p - x)` as one statement, and `sf` recomputed from the raw input every bar. Each EMA is called once per rule, in the hook. Where a note says only "MA", it is an EMA (steering), except P2, whose spec states SMAs. Lagged reads `ema[k]` are the measured series history (`XAVERAGE_LAG_FINDINGS.md`). The C++ mirrors each one with a zero-initialized ring buffer that re-seeds on a depth change, and every rule reading `ema[k]` is guarded on `CurrentBar` and never compares against the zero pre-history. Containment tests against a computed EMA are written as separate `<=`/`>=` comparisons, not `MaxList`/`MinList`, because those two words are ACCEPTED only for tick-grid price operands.

| `MaDistanceRegime` | Entry | mirrored | `max(longWindow - 1, shortWindow - 1, atrLookback + 1)` | `emaLength(50)`, `longWindow(100)`, `shortWindow(10)`, `minLongAtr(1.0)`, `maxShortAtr(0.25)`, `atrLookback(20)` | Pane 1. `dist = Close − EMA`. | `EmaTouchAfterLongAbsence` | Entry | mirrored | `maxScanBars` (plus the recursive EMA state) | `emaLength(200)`, `gapMultiple(3)`, `priorGaps(5)`, `maxScanBars(500)` | Pane 1. This bar touches the long EMA (`Low <= ema <= High`), and the gap since the previous touch is at least `gapMultiple` × the mean of the `priorGaps` gaps before it. The trade goes THROUGH the average, expecting it not to hold: LONG when the prior bar was entirely below the EMA (`High[1] < ema[1]`), SHORT when entirely above. A gap is the distance in bars between successive touch bars. Arrays are unregistered, so instead of the spec's ring of gaps the touch history is a scan back from bar 1, capped at `maxScanBars` and at `CurrentBar − 1`. The first touch found gives the current gap; the mean of the earlier gaps telescopes to (the `priorGaps + 1`-th touch back − the first) / `priorGaps`. With fewer than `priorGaps + 1` touches inside the scan there is no signal. The note's K/N are renamed `gapMultiple`/`priorGaps`; `maxScanBars` is new. Not `TwentyGapBarPullbackEntry`, which uses a FIXED gap and buys the hold, nor `EmaGapBarRun`, which has no direction or adaptive threshold. Source: Brian's notes (ON-85-a, #85). |
