# Slice 04_bar_shape: CATALOG rows

`LargeBarTightPauseNearHigh`, `LargeBarHoldThenCloseThroughMidpoint`, `LargeBarAtExtremeNextBarExtendsWeakClose` and `HugeBarOppositeInsideBarThenResume` inline one form of the shared primitive ON-12-a (`LargeShapedBarAtLag`), with the same local names in each: range strictly between `minRangeAtr` and `maxRangeAtr` × the ATR AS OF THAT BAR, open in the bottom `openFraction` of its range and close in the top `closeFraction` (bear: open top, close bottom). The lagged ATR is EL `atrValue[lag]`, which reads the variable's own zero-filled history. The C++ mirrors that history with a growing `push_back` vector (the `StochasticCross` form), so a mid-run input change never discards it. `LargeBarInsideRunCloseBeyond`, `BearBarTopHalfUntouchedLong` and `BreakawayPullbackBreak` read their lagged ATR the same way. Every ATR is `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange` in the rolling form (`SwingLowRejection`'s block, double-placement safe).
