# Slice 08_price_visits: CATALOG rows

All seven rules inline the same visit scan, primitive P1 (`PriceVisitScan`, TRIAGE "P1 rules", definition confirmed by Brian), with the same local and input names in each: a VISIT is a bar `kk` bars ago, `minGap` <= `kk` <= `lookback`, whose `Low..High` range contains the CURRENT close, inclusive (EL `Low[kk] <= Close and High[kk] >= Close`, C++ `el_le`/`el_ge`). No ATR band. Every rule except `LevelThinAir` requires at least `minVisits` visits (and at least one, so no mean divides by zero; EL raises on a zero denominator, so every mean is guarded with `if count > 0`). It is a difference test, so it passes the shift test. The loop reads `lookback` bars back unconditionally, so Max Bars Back must be at least `lookback` (500 by default). Every ATR is the CURRENT `WFSafe_AvgTrueRange` in the rolling form (`SwingLowRejection`'s block, double-placement safe), one ATR for every visit.

| Rule | Type | Sides | Reaches back | Inputs | Fires when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LevelRejectedFromAbove` | Entry | mirrored | `lookback` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `minVisits(5)`, `wickFraction(0.6)` | Pane 1. The P1 visit scan finds at least `minVisits` past bars (between `minGap` and `lookback` bars ago) whose range contains the current close. An UPPER-WICK visit has the close strictly above the visit's body top (`MaxList(Open, Close)`), so between body top and high. A LOWER-WICK visit has it strictly below the body bottom. SHORT when at least `wickFraction` of the visits are upper-wick visits, meaning the level was rejected from above. LONG mirrors on lower-wick visits. A body visit, edges included, is neither, which makes the wick tests the strict complement of `LevelBodyDominated`. `MaxList`/`MinList` select between open and close, tick-grid prices within their ACCEPTED scope, so the C++ uses plain comparisons (`AverageUpperTailFraction`'s form). No library rule classifies past bars at the current price. Not `LevelTouchCount`, which counts touches of one pivot level. Source: Brian's notes (P1a, P1). |
| `LevelForwardDrift` | Entry | mirrored | `max(lookback, atrLookback + 1)` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `minVisits(5)`, `horizon(10)`, `driftAtr(0.5)`, `atrLookback(14)` | Pane 1. The P1 visit scan counts only visits whose `horizon`-bar forward window is complete and strictly in the past (`kk > horizon`, so the window ends at bar 1 at the latest and the current bar is never in it). Each visit's forward move is `Close[kk − horizon] − Close[kk]`, measured from the visit bar's close (the spec's reading, not from the current close). LONG when at least `minVisits` such visits exist and their mean forward move is at least `driftAtr` × the CURRENT ATR (`WFSafe_AvgTrueRange(atrLookback)` on this bar, the steering's "current is simplest"). SHORT when the mean is at most −`driftAtr` × that ATR. The `minVisits` floor applies to the forward-complete visits, the sample the mean is taken over. With the defaults (`minGap` 20 > `horizon` 10), every visit qualifies. Source: Brian's notes (P1b, P1). |
| `LevelThinAir` | Entry | mirrored | `max(lookback, momentumLookback)` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `maxVisits(2)`, `momentumLookback(10)` | Pane 1. Price is in thin air when the P1 visit scan finds AT MOST `maxVisits` visits. There is no `minVisits` floor, since zero visits is the purest thin air and a floor at the other defaults (5 > 2) would make the rule unreachable. Entered in the direction of the last `momentumLookback`-bar move: LONG when `Close > Close[momentumLookback]`, SHORT when below. The visit count is identical on both sides. The direction half is `Momentum(momentumLookback)`; it is kept inside as TRIAGE's pane-1 rule. Also covers 20250524.1's "vertical roadmap". Source: Brian's notes (P1c, P1). |
| `LevelBodyDominated` | Entry | same | `lookback` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `minVisits(5)`, `bodyFraction(0.6)` | Pane 2. Of at least `minVisits` P1 visits, at least `bodyFraction` had the current close inside the visit's body, `MinList(Open, Close) <= Close <= MaxList(Open, Close)` with the edges included. Identical both sides. The strict complement of `LevelRejectedFromAbove`'s two wick classes. Source: Brian's notes (P1d, P1). |
| `LevelWasTrending` | Entry | same | `max(lookback, adxLength)` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `minVisits(5)`, `adxLength(14)`, `adxThreshold(25)` | Pane 2. Of at least `minVisits` P1 visits, the mean ADX on the visit bars is at least `adxThreshold`. Negate the placement for "at most", the mirror in TRIAGE. Each visit's ADX is its value AS OF THAT BAR. EL computes `adxValue = WFSafe_ADX(adxLength)` every bar and reads `adxValue[kk]`, the VARIABLE's own history, which reads 0 before the strategy's first evaluated bar (the CLAUDE.md zero-fill convention, and the slice-04 `atrValue[lag]` form). So visits older than the evaluated history contribute 0 to the mean, as in EL. The C++ is `AdxBelowThreshold`'s verified chain verbatim (input renamed `adxLength`), with its history mirrored as a growing `push_back` vector (`StochasticCross`'s form). Not `AdxBelowThreshold`, which reads the current ADX only. Source: Brian's notes (P1e, P1). |
| `LevelBigBars` | Entry | same | `max(lookback, atrLookback + 1)` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `minVisits(5)`, `rangeAtr(1.2)`, `atrLookback(14)` | Pane 2. Of at least `minVisits` P1 visits, the mean `High − Low` range of the visit bars is at least `rangeAtr` × the CURRENT ATR. Negate the placement for small bars. Identical both sides. One current ATR is used, not each visit's own, so the measure reads "how big were the bars here, in today's volatility units". Not `BarRangeAtrMultiple` or `LargeBarCountInWindow`, which test recent bars rather than the bars at the current price. Source: Brian's notes (P1f, P1). |
| `LevelHighVolume` | Entry | same | `max(lookback, volumeLookback - 1)` | `minGap(20)`, `lookback(500)`, `minVisits(5)`, `volumeMultiple(1.2)`, `volumeLookback(50)` | Pane 2. Of at least `minVisits` P1 visits, the mean TOTAL ticks of the visit bars (`Ticks[kk]`, up + down, not the up-tick `Volume` series) is at least `volumeMultiple` × the `volumeLookback`-bar average of `vol = Ticks`. The average is computed exactly as `VolumeAboveAverageMultiple` computes it, over the variable's zero-filled warm-up history with a newest-first ring buffer. The visit bars' ticks are the data series itself, with real history. Negate the placement for low volume. Identical both sides. TRIAGE's "the same rule on volume", split from `LevelBigBars` to keep one measure per rule. Source: Brian's notes (P1f-b, P1). |
